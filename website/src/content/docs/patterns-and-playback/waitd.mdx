---
name: waitd
params: [play, value]
arities: [2]
variadic: false
receives: play
returns: play
---

[wait](#wait), in a written note value instead of bars:
`playn(verse, lead, 4).waitd(h)` waits half a bar in 4/4.

It takes the values a pattern is written in — `w`, `h`, `q`, `e`, `s`, dotted
with `q.dot`, added together with `h + e` — and how much of a bar one comes to
follows the time signature. A half note is half a 4/4 bar and two thirds of a
3/4 one.

A plain number is refused rather than guessed at: `waitd(2)` has two readings
and the language has a name for each. Write [wait](#wait) for bars or
[waits](#waits) for seconds.

[wait](#wait), in seconds instead of bars: `playn(verse, lead, 4).waits(1.5)`.

The seconds are converted to bars at the tempo the program was played at, so
changing the tempo afterwards stretches the gap along with the music. That is
the right answer for something sitting in an arrangement — a gap that stayed
1.5 seconds while the bars around it got longer would walk the piece off the
grid — and it is why `waits` is a convenience for writing a length down rather
than a promise about wall time.

How long a bar is follows the time signature as well as the tempo, so at 120
bpm two seconds is one bar in 4/4 and three quarters of one in 3/4.

The gap cannot be negative.

---
name: wait
params: [play, bars]
arities: [2]
variadic: false
receives: play
returns: play
---

Hold a chain open for `bars` of silence before anything follows:
`playn(verse, lead, 4).wait(2).then(chorus)` leaves the chorus two bars after
the verse ends.

What is already playing is untouched — only the next section moves, the same
way [quantize](#quantize) rounds the join without shortening the notes.

[then_after](#then_after) says the same thing about one join, and needs a
section in hand to say it. A wait is a link in the chain instead, so it reads
in the order it happens and composes with everything else that chains —
`.loop` repeats the gap along with the notes:

```swync
playn(groove, kit, 2).wait(2).loop(3)
```

is two bars on and two bars off, three times through.

The gap cannot be negative. To start the next section *early*, reach for
[overlap](#overlap).

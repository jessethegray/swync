---
name: then_after
params: [play, bars, section]
arities: [3]
variadic: false
receives: play
returns: play
---

`then`, with `bars` of silence in between the previous play: 
`playn(verse, lead, 4).then_after(1, chorus)` leaves a bar's rest before the chorus.
The gap cannot be negative.

[then_afters](#then_afters) writes the same gap in seconds and
[then_afterd](#then_afterd) in a written note value; [wait](#wait) writes one
without a section in hand, as a link in the chain.

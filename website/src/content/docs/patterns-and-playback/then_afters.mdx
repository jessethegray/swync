[then_after](#then_after), in seconds instead of bars:
`playn(verse, lead, 4).then_afters(1.5, chorus)` leaves a second and a half
before the chorus.

The seconds are converted to bars at the tempo the program was played at, so
changing the tempo afterwards stretches the gap along with the music — the
same bargain [waits](#waits) makes, and for the same reason. How long a bar is
follows the time signature too, so at 120 bpm two seconds is one bar in 4/4
and three quarters of one in 3/4.

The gap cannot be negative; [overlap](#overlap) is how a section starts early.
